Best Places to Have Dinner in Cox's Bazar

From smoky live open-air fish fry markets on Sugandha beach to candle-lit oceanfront cabanas along Marine Drive.

BEACHFRONT LIVE FRY

📍 Directly on Sugandha Beach

As dusk falls, dozens of stalls light up along the sands. Choose your own raw tuna, squid, crab, coral, or lobster on ice, negotiate the rate, and have it grilled over live coals right before your eyes.

✨ Tip: Agree on the price per piece before cooking!

INSIDER GUIDANCE

Tips for Eating Well & Safely in Cox's Bazar

1. Selecting Fresh Fish

When picking fish at Sugandha live stalls, ensure the eyes are crystal clear (not cloudy), the flesh springs back when pressed, and the gills underneath are bright crimson.

2. Clarifying Spice Levels

Coastal curries and bhartas can be fiery hot. If you prefer milder food, tell your server clearly: "Jhaal kom deben" (Please make it less spicy).

3. Transparent Billing

Traditional rice restaurants charge separately for rice, lentils, and each plate of bharta (৳30–৳60 each). At beach fry stalls, confirm the total cooking fee before frying starts.
