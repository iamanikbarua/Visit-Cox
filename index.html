TRAVELER BRIEFING

Seasons, Safety & Etiquette

Whether you are visiting for the first time or returning, knowing seasonal weather patterns and beach safety rules ensures an unforgettable coastal trip.

Nov — Feb (Golden Season)

Calm, turquoise sea, pleasant 22°C–28°C weather, perfect for Marine Drive drives and boat rides to Maheshkhali.

Jun — Aug (Monsoon Drama)

Huge rolling waves, dramatic skies, and 50% hotel discounts. Swimming is strictly prohibited during red flags.

Beach Safety Guidelines

🟢 Green Flag Calm water; safe swimming within 50 meters of the marked lifeguard post.
🟡 Yellow Flag Moderate swell & riptides. Exercise extreme caution; stay knee-deep.
🔴 Red Flag Danger. High tide undertow. Absolutely no swimming permitted by police.
