SAFETY & OPERATOR PROTOCOLS

Important Guidelines for Coastal Activities

1. Water Sports Lifejackets

Always ensure your lifejacket is CE-certified and securely buckled before boarding parasailing speedboats, jet skis, or channel crossing trawlers. Do not hesitate to request a properly fitting vest.

2. Obeying Beach Flags

CIPRB Sea Safe Lifeguards patrol Laboni, Sugandha, and Kolatoli. Swim and surf strictly between green/yellow flags. Never enter the water during red flag high-current warnings.

3. Marine Drive Safari Vehicles

When hiring an open-top Chander Gari, book through your hotel desk or inspect the tires and seatbelts at Kolatoli Circle. Agree on the total roundtrip fare including wait times at Inani and Himchari.
