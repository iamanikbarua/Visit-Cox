## 2. Marine Drive Motorcycle & Scooter Rentals
* **100cc – 125cc Scooter (Scooty):** ৳১,০০০ – ৳১,২০০ / দিন (8:00 AM – 7:00 PM)
* **150cc Motorcycle (Pulsar / FZ / Gixxer):** ৳১,২০০ – ৳১,৫০০ / দিন
* **Requirements:** National ID Card (NID) copy, original driving license or student ID as security deposit.
* **Fuel:** Self-pay (Octane rate approx. ৳১২৫ – ৳১৩০/liter). A full round trip to Inani requires ~2-3 liters.

## 3. Tourist Scam Prevention Rules
1. **Never board without agreeing on fare first:** Always ask *"ভাড়া কত?"* before entering a CNG or reserved Tomtom.
2. **Clarify "Reserve" vs "Local Share":** A driver saying *"চলুন উঠে পড়েন"* may assume you are reserving the entire vehicle.
3. **Evening Return Surcharge:** When returning from Inani or Himchari after sunset (after 6:30 PM), CNGs usually demand ৳১০০–৳২০০ extra due to empty return trips.
