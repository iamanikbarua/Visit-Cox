What Visit Cox is

A rough trip budget and fair local prices, before you go

For first-time visitors to the world's longest sea beach: where to stay, what to see, what to eat, how to get around and what each part should cost. Every page works in English and Bangla, in taka or US dollars.

Honest numbers

Prices, fares and hotel rates are labelled as estimates with an asOf date. Emergency numbers carry their source.

No server

Plain HTML and JavaScript on GitHub Pages. No login, no database, no admin panel, nothing sent anywhere by the planner.

Built for phones

Every page collapses to one column; the planner exports a phone lock-screen pass for the trip.

11 pages · 16 hotels · 14 sights + 10 hidden gems · 10 activities · 6 fare routes · 8 FAQs · 4 blog posts · 51 tests run by scripts/check.sh.
